Reaching the summit of excellence, from wherever you are.

Kenya Pinnacle School is a virtual school following the Kenyan Competency-Based Curriculum for Grades 2 to 12, with live classes, virtual labs and a digital library.

Learning levels

Each learner sees a portal built for their stage, from simple audio-guided activities to pathway-based senior school.

Grades 2 to 3

Lower Primary

Literacy and numeracy activity tracks with a simple layout and audio prompts.

Grades 4 to 6

Upper Primary

Science, Agriculture, Creative Arts and Social Studies with guided exploration.

Grades 7 to 9

Junior School

Broad-based learning including Integrated Science, Pre-Technical Studies and Business Studies.

Grades 10 to 12

Senior School

Pathways in STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts and Sports Science.

How learning works

Everything a learner, teacher and parent needs sits in one place.

Live Google Meet classes

A timetable with quick-join links. Attendance is recorded when learners join through the portal.

Competency tracking

Progress shown as Exceeding, Meeting, Approaching or Below expectations, based on continuous assessment.

Assignments and portfolio

Submit work online and build a digital portfolio that teachers can comment on.

Parent view

Follow attendance, progress and fee balances in one dashboard.

Virtual labs and digital library

Practical learning without a physical lab, plus a searchable library sorted by grade and subject.

Science labs

Titration, circuits, mechanics, dissection, ecosystems and microscope slides.

Geography

Map reading, landscape tools and weather tracking.

Languages

English, Kiswahili and other languages with pronunciation and grammar practice.

Music

Digital piano, traditional instruments and rhythm training.

Library

E-books, revision papers, videos, quizzes and audio kits filtered by grade, subject and type.
